    About this map

    Hull PorchFest 2026 is a free afternoon of live music on porches, yards and driveways around Hull. It takes place on Saturday, September 19, 2026, from 1 PM to 6 PM. This map lists 38 acts on 15 porches. The official site is hullporchfest.com.

    How to use it

    • Search for a band, a genre or a street, or pick a genre.
    • Playing now shows who is on right now. Pick a time lets you plan ahead; press Play the day to watch the afternoon unfold.
    • The list follows the map: zoom in on your neighborhood to see what is nearby, or turn off “Only list porches in the map view”.
    • Open a set in the list and choose Show on map. Tap a marker to see everything at that porch.
    • On a phone, drag or tap the bar above the list to make it bigger or smaller.

    Colors

    Green markers are playing at the chosen time, blue are coming up and purple are done. Wider markers have more sets.
